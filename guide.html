DIY project guide

Make a ribbon door hanger or table centerpiece

Ribbon loops, airy tulle and the accents you choose. One stick, about ten steps.

Steps 1 to 4

Start your project

  1. Gather the supplies on the back panel.
  2. Heat the glue gun and keep a glue stick handy.
  3. Lay the EZ Craft Stick flat on your work surface, short tabs on the right.
4

Make the hanging loop

Door or wall hanging only. Make a 2-inch loop from the wide ribbon and hot glue it to the top of the stick. Making a centerpiece? Skip to step 5.

Your stick: 4 short tabs, 3 tall tabs

Step 5

Form the first loop

Pull the wide ribbon about 4 inches back over your fingers, then bring it forward again to make a loop. Make it longer or shorter for the look you want.

First loop at the top tab

Step 6

Tuck and secure

Tuck both ribbon edges under the top tab and angle the loop slightly to one side. Fold the edges back, run a line of hot glue underneath, then fold the ribbon back into place and hold it while the glue cools.

  1. A

    Tuck both edges under the top tab.

  2. B

    Angle the loop and fold back the ribbon edges.

  3. C

    Fold the ribbon back into place and hold it as the glue cools.

EZCraft Stick4

Step 7

Alternate and trim

Repeat at each remaining tab, switching sides every time. Hot glue the ribbon at the bottom of the stick. For a door hanging, trim flush or leave a tail for the bow. For a centerpiece, trim flush with the end.

  1. A

    Make the next loop on the opposite side.

  2. B

    Hot glue the ribbon at the bottom.

  3. C

    Trim the ribbon to suit your project.

One loop per tab, alternating

Step 8

Weave in the tulle

Glue one end of the tulle to the bottom of the stick. Weave it back and forth between the ribbon loops. Even out both sides, tack the center of the tulle to the tabs between loops, then fluff it.

  1. A

    Anchor the tulle at the bottom.

  2. B

    Weave the tulle between the ribbon loops.

  3. C

    Even out both sides and fluff the tulle.

Tulle weaves between loops

Steps 9 and 10

Decorate and finish

9

Arrange and attach accents

Lay out the flowers, stems or ornaments first. When you like the arrangement, hot glue them in place.

10

Finish your project

Door hanging: glue the bow to the bottom and hang it by the loop. Centerpiece: lay the finished piece on the table.

Before you start

Gather your supplies

  • 1 EZ Craft Stick
  • 2½ yards of wired, double-sided ribbon, 2 to 3 inches wide
  • 1½ yards of tulle, 6 inches wide
  • Hot glue gun with 2 or 3 glue sticks
  • Scissors
  • 7 to 12 flowers, stems or ornaments
  • For a door hanger: a 4 to 8 inch bow, or 1½ yards of ribbon to make your own

Choose your project

Door or wall hanging. Add a hanging loop at the top and a bow at the bottom.

Table centerpiece. Trim the ribbon flush at both ends. Lay two end to end for a runner.

Craft use only. Never use this stick to install or secure a child car seat or seat belt. Hot glue and the glue gun tip get very hot, so kids should craft with an adult.
