This is the stick

A shallow black channel with seven tabs. The tabs lift just enough to slide ribbon underneath, then grip it while the glue sets.

Length
14 in
Width
2.4 in
Tabs
7, alternating sides
Material
Black plastic, reusable

How it works

Six steps. Tap a step to see it on the stick.

Step 1 of 6

Lay the stick flat

Short tabs run down one side and tall tabs down the other. Each tab holds one fold of ribbon.

What you need
  • 1 EZ Craft Stick
  • 2½ yards of wired, double-sided ribbon, 2 to 3 inches wide
  • 1½ yards of tulle, 6 inches wide
  • Hot glue gun and 2 or 3 glue sticks
  • Scissors
  • 7 to 12 flowers, stems or ornaments
  • For a hanging piece, a 4 to 8 inch bow

It started as a car seat tool

The stick was first sold as EZ-CarSeat®, a tool that guides a seat belt through the back of a child's car seat. The same tabs that hold a seat belt in place turned out to hold a loop of ribbon just as well.

So we gave it a second job. Same stick, new purpose.

Do not use this to install a car seat

The EZ Craft Stick is sold only as a craft tool. It has not been tested or certified for child passenger safety, and we are not selling it for that purpose. Never use it to install, adjust or secure a child car seat or seat belt. Follow your car seat and vehicle manufacturer's instructions, or ask a certified child passenger safety technician for help.
